At IISc, I build sequence models that turn radar Pulse Descriptor Word trains into per-pulse embeddings for multi-emitter separation. The work combines Transformer encoders, metric learning, HDBSCAN evaluation, and multi-object tracking with reproducible multi-GPU training pipelines.
